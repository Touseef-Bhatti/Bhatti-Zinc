Packaging & Shipping

Packed for every trade route

We offer flexible packaging and container load configurations for every product, optimised for cost efficiency and cargo safety.

FCL 20ft

Standard container — 18–22 MT depending on product and packing format.

FCL 40ft

Full 40-foot container — up to 26 MT for bulk or high-volume orders.

LCL Shipment

Less-than-container-load for smaller orders. Minimum order quantities apply.

Bulk Vessel

Air Freight
