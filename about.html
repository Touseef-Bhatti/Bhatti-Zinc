Our Story

From Pakistan to
the world

Building strength in the zinc industry since 1998, BhattiZinc began as a local trading business in Gujranwala and has since transformed into a trusted manufacturer, importer, exporter, and supplier of premium zinc products for national and international markets.

Under the leadership of our Director, Asees Bhatti, BhattiZinc has grown on a foundation of quality, transparency and long-term partnerships. Our focus on reliable supply, competitive pricing and consistent product purity has helped us build durable relationships with galvanizers, die-casters, chemical manufacturers and construction companies worldwide.

We are committed to sustainable recycling, modern smelting practices and international compliance. Our certifications and documented quality systems ensure every shipment is accompanied by full Certificates of Analysis and the documentation required by importers across multiple continents.

At BhattiZinc we believe progress is powered by integrity — from local roots to global reach, powered by trust, quality and consistency.

Frequently Asked Questions

About BhattiZinc
and our zinc products

BhattiZinc is a Pakistan-based zinc manufacturer, recycler, exporter and supplier serving industrial buyers worldwide. Find direct answers about our products, quality documentation, sourcing and export support.

What does BhattiZinc supply?

BhattiZinc supplies zinc metal, zinc ash, zinc oxide, zinc ingots, zinc alloys, zinc sheets, zinc wire and other zinc-based materials for galvanizing, die casting, chemical manufacturing, construction and industrial applications.

Where is BhattiZinc located?

BhattiZinc operates from Gujranwala, Pakistan, where its zinc recycling, manufacturing, laboratory testing and export operations support customers in Pakistan and international markets.

What is the difference between zinc metal and zinc ash?

Zinc metal is a metallic zinc feedstock supplied in forms such as ingots or other agreed specifications. Zinc ash is a zinc-containing by-product recovered from industrial processes. The right material depends on the buyer’s process, required zinc content, recovery method and quality specification.

Can BhattiZinc provide zinc oxide for industrial use?

Yes. BhattiZinc manufactures zinc oxide for industrial buyers and can discuss the required grade, packaging, application, quantity and documentation before confirming a quotation.

Does BhattiZinc provide certificates of analysis?

BhattiZinc provides product quality documentation, including Certificates of Analysis where applicable, to help buyers verify product chemistry and compare the material with their purchasing specification. Request the relevant report when sending an inquiry.

What industries use BhattiZinc products?

BhattiZinc serves galvanizers, die casters, brass and zinc alloy manufacturers, chemical producers, construction-related businesses and other industrial users that require dependable zinc feedstock or zinc compounds.

Does BhattiZinc export zinc products internationally?

Yes. BhattiZinc manages international export orders from Pakistan and supports buyers with product specifications, commercial documentation, packing details, shipment coordination and the information needed for import planning.

How can I request a zinc product quotation?

Use the BhattiZinc contact page to share the product name, required specification or zinc content, quantity, packing preference, destination country and target delivery schedule. The sales team can then confirm availability and prepare the next steps.
