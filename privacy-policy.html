Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to BhattiZinc. This page explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when using our website.

BhattiZinc Industries (Pvt) Ltd. respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personal information collected through our website. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how you can control it.

Information We Collect

We collect information when you interact with our website, including when you submit a contact or career form, request a quote, or use WhatsApp application links. This may include:

  • Contact details such as name, email address, phone number, and company name.
  • Enquiry details including product interests, quantities, application messages, and preferred service requirements.
  • Device and browsing information such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited, which helps us improve website performance.

How We Use Your Information

We use information collected from our website for the following purposes:

  • To respond to enquiries and quote requests.
  • To process career applications and contact applicants regarding vacancies.
  • To improve our website, products, and customer service.
  • To provide news, updates, or information you request, where permitted by law.

WhatsApp Communication

When you submit a form that opens WhatsApp, the data you provide is formatted into a message and sent through the WhatsApp service. BhattiZinc does not control WhatsApp privacy practices; please review WhatsAppâ€™s privacy policies for details on their data handling.

Cookies and Tracking

Our website may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve navigation and analyze usage. Cookies help us understand how visitors engage with the site, enabling us to improve the experience. You can manage cookie preferences directly in your browser settings.

Data Sharing and Third Parties

BhattiZinc does not sell personal information. We may share data with third-party service providers only as necessary to support website operations, communication, or legal requirements. These providers are obligated to protect your information.

Security

We maintain reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information from unauthorized access and disclosure. However, no online system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute protection.

Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

Your Rights

Where applicable, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. If you wish to make such a request, please contact us using the details on our Contact page.

Changes to this Policy

BhattiZinc may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The revised policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact

For questions about this policy or our privacy practices, please email info@bhattizinc.com.
