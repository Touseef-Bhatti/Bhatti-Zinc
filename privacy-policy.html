Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to BhattiZinc. This page explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when using our website.

BhattiZinc Industries (Pvt) Ltd. respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personal information collected through our website. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how you can control it.

We collect information when you interact with our website, including when you submit a contact or career form, request a quote, or use WhatsApp application links. This may include:
