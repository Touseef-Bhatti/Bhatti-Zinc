Material Insight

Industrial Uses of Zinc Oxide Powder and How to Choose a Supplier

Zinc oxide is one of the most searched zinc chemicals because it appears in rubber, ceramics, coatings, feed and chemical manufacturing. Buyers need purity, particle consistency and reliable packing.

Rubber and tire applications

Zinc oxide is a key material in rubber compounding, where it helps vulcanization systems perform properly. Consistent ZnO quality supports predictable curing and finished product performance.

Ceramics, coatings and chemicals

In ceramics and coatings, zinc oxide can support opacity, stability and functional properties. Chemical producers also use ZnO as a zinc source for downstream zinc compounds.

Supplier checks for ZnO buyers

Ask for purity, whiteness, moisture, packing format and batch documentation. For export markets, SDS, compliance paperwork and palletized packing can reduce receiving delays.
