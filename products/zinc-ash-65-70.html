Material Insight

Zinc Ash Recovery: Turning Galvanizing By-Products into Value

Zinc ash, also searched as zinc skimmings or galvanizing ash, is valuable because it contains recoverable zinc. High-grade ash can improve recovery economics for recyclers and smelters.

Why zinc ash has recycling value

During galvanizing and zinc processing, ash forms on molten zinc surfaces. Material with 65-70% zinc content gives recovery plants a stronger zinc-bearing feedstock than lower-grade residues.

Common buyers and applications

Zinc ash is purchased by smelters, metal recovery plants and secondary zinc producers. It can be processed to reclaim metallic zinc or used as feedstock for zinc compounds depending on chemistry.

What to check before purchase

Buyers should review zinc content, moisture, contamination level, particle form, packing and shipment size. Accurate sampling is important because recovery yield depends on real composition.

Procurement Notes

What is zinc ash used for?

Zinc ash is mainly used for zinc recovery, recycling, smelting and secondary zinc production.

Is 65-70% zinc ash high grade?

Yes, 65-70% Zn is considered a strong recovery-grade material compared with lower zinc ash grades.
